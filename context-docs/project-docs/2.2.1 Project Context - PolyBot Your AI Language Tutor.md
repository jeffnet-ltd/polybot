## Recent Updates (v2.2.1 - 2026-04-18)

### A1.4.BOSS Conversation Practice — Three Bug Fixes ✅

**Lesson:** "Conversation Practice: The Trattoria Dinner" — single-round restaurant ordering scenario.

#### Bug #1: Conditional Drink Variant Turn ✅

**Problem:** Turn 2 always responded with a generic "Perfetto. E per mangiare?" regardless of what drink the student ordered.

**Fix:** Turn 2 is now a conditional turn with drink-specific AI response variants:

| Student orders | AI responds |
|---|---|
| Acqua (water) | "Naturale o frizzante?" |
| Vino (wine) | "Rosso o bianco?" |
| Birra (beer) | "Perfetto. E per mangiare?" (skips sub-question) |

**Implementation:** Added `"conditional": True`, `"condition": "drink_type"`, and `"ai_message_variants"` dict to Turn 2 in `backend/a1_4_module_data.py`.

#### Bug #2: Single-Round Completion Detection ✅

**Problem:** The conversation was not detecting completion correctly — it expected multiple rounds but the lesson is designed as a single round.

**Fix:** Updated completion logic to detect end-of-meal signals (dessert/coffee orders, payment requests) within a single round and mark the scene as `COMPLETE`.

#### Bug #3: Turn 2 Validation & Turn Counter Alignment ✅

**Problem:** Turn 2 validation was failing for valid water-type responses ("naturale", "frizzante") and the turn counter was misaligned after the conditional branch.

**Fix:** Added `"required_words": ["naturale", "frizzante", "rosso", "bianco"]` to Turn 2 and corrected the turn counter increment logic so subsequent turns align correctly.

**File Modified:** `backend/a1_4_module_data.py`

**Test documentation:** `IMPLEMENTATION_SUMMARY.md`, `TEST_SCENARIOS.md` (project root)
